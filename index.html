Statement of Record

Fair Rights and Access documents, organizes, and preserves records connected to ongoing legal and court-related matters.

This is a website dedicated to documenting, organizing, and preserving a factual record of events that are directly connected to ongoing legal and court-related matters affecting my life, my work, and my ability to defend myself.

This is not a casual or personal project. The issues presented here arise from disputes that have entered, or are connected to, formal legal processes, where my rights, my ideas, and my identity have been challenged through patterns of deceit, misrepresentation, and fraudulent conduct.

In multiple instances, I have faced barriers to full participation—whether through limited access to information, restricted visibility into accusations, or conditions that prevent a complete and timely defense. These circumstances raise serious concerns regarding transparency and procedural fairness.

By maintaining this archive, I assert my right to document, to be heard, and to ensure that no part of this record can be obscured, altered, or selectively presented without accountability.

-Joo-yeon Kim
