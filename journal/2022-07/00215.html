After all those attacks in the public,

behind my back, gaslighting in person,

breaching my privacy entering my room,

trying to humiliate me in public,

trying to distort the image of my character,

while I was living in this house,

despite the warnings, persistent.

Passive aggression, even insinuating violence.

All worked up by themselves,

and given evidence of abuse in the public,

 

 

and they were dismissed?

they were not charged at all?

 

 

While I was fully paying the rent,

minding my own business?

 

 

Ok, something is wrong here as well.
