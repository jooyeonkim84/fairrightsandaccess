you can understand how i feel,

 

and how violated i feel of all this.

to protect my individual rights.

in my defense.

use this as an opportunity,

 

and will repeat harm.

 

 

 

whether authorities in charge are allowing them

to eventually address them, to throw a punchline,

 

or just allowing them dismissing it as their

individual 'rights', i have no idea.

 

but you would know, some of these people are

fully attempting harm, whichever way.

 

 

i have to focus on survival/sustenance.

 

but it bothers me greatly, if my image even shopping

at groceries, is being released into the public, LIVE.

 

and people take advantage of the situation,

to gain something out of it.

 

they would want to catch attention,

and use me as a stepping stone for controversy.

 

the longer this is allowed,

the more harm it will create.

 

 

and note that i am not exactly in a situation

where i can do anything about it.

 

 

but you can.

 

i hope you can address these people.

 

 

and sorry i am writing in a frustrating tone.

but when it becomes relentless,

when the harm doesn't seem to stop,

and this is to convey a message.

 

that i need help.

to address these people.

and continue doing.
