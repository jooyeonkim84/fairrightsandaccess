Looks like everyone is not treating me like a person.

 

 

Take away my rights.

 

Dismiss all the harm that was done on me

as if it were NOTHING.

 

 

While these people tried destroying my life.

 

 

My mind, my life.

 

 

Whereas I tried forgiving them,

 

they attacked again as I rose in power.

 

 

 

And again, as I tried to forgive another time.

 

Attacked in the process of this. Trying to frame me.

 

 

 

And they were let go?

 

That would be serious corruption.
