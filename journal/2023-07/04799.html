Smug, gloating stance.

 

Over my mother's death,

 

and our attempt to survive.

 

 

You don't incarcerate them.

 

 

Then what am I suppose to see?

 

 

 

That fucking bitch.

 

 

Religious belief topples over another person's belief,

 

in their critical hours. And it thinks it has every right.

 

It still gloats.

 

 

You understand?
