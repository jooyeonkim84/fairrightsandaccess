There are people,

 

who began from a place of harm.

 

To use these people to trick me,

 

to affect me in some way,

 

and they happily joining in, in the process of this.

 

 

Is but continued pursuit of harm, it is felony.

 

 

To ignore the history and origins of their harm,

 

in fact, to have used this very fact,

 

that in it, itself is manipulation.

 

 

 

Whether starting, or joining. This is intended harm.

 

 

 

I am not here to be toyed with.

 

 

 

Whatever that was dismissed as charges,

 

must be revived.
