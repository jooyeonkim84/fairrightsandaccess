i used to believe in the idea that,

 

love comes only once in life,

 

whether at the highest of reach of our senses, in our youth,

 

or that innocence we still had back when capable of belief in this.

 

 

 

but. without being treacherous to one's own loyalty to that idea of love.

 

every love is different, every love is another chance.

 

and if you can grow it, it only grows stronger.

 

 

 

so. Ariko. It's only a matter of opening up your heart.

 

 

I wish you the best in your journey.

 

 

It was an honor you thought of me in that way, that it was mutual,

for a long time.
