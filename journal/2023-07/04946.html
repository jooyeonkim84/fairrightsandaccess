it feels exactly like,

 

i am being treated like a child,

 

one people can manipulate and laugh at 'out of good will'.

 

 

whereas in reality, this is exactly of that, harassment.

 

and the bar was set so low,

 

you no longer can recognize the difference.

 

 

 

you do realize where this began from, and where i am at

continued from there.

 

your actions, and what you say will be used against you.

 

the many of you.

 

 

 

and further, what are you people doing

 

with trans, queer, gay, bi crap?

 

when i state i am not,

 

with no disrespect to people who are,

 

i simply am not.

 

 

opinion could be allowed,

 

but not from those people,

 

whose intent has been harm,

 

and has engaged in slander

 

at any given point.

 

 

and why are they simply let go?

 

 

you just wait on this controversy to grow,

 

thinking it will benefit me in the long run?

 

 

what kind of fame do you think i want?

 

one that deviates away from my identity?

 

 

this is insanity.

 

 

stop allowing me to be targeted.
