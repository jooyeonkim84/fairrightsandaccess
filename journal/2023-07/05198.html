In a way, your ideas on this exchange works the same way

for me.

 

 

1. 'I' was the one to give you opportunities,

as you have thought the throne was an 'opportunity' for me.

 

2. 'I' was the one who was testing your integrity.

Carefully instructing and being patient with your choices.

 

3. 'I' was the one adopting yourselves as 'family'.

It's vice-versa. Never single way.

 

 

So you too, have thought this from an arrogant, privileged place.

You've made errors that dismantles trust. You stood alongside

people who are clearly abusive, with no particular 'evidence'

to their claims but their prejudice. You allow it to go on.

 

And at your amusement.

 

 

And when things don't go your way, I see you taunting as well.

 

 

You think you are forgivable?

 

 

 

We are at an end. Here.

 

 

 

If you have integrity, you will clean this up,

still knowing we have fallen apart.

 

 

And that is all.

 

 

Indifference follows after this, while keeping

up to the end of my bargain.
