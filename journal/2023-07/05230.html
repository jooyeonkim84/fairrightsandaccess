people asking mercy.

 

that is for accidents.

 

not deliberate choice to affect.

 

then there is no humanity. no deserving mercy.

 

 

 

there are things without remorse, without conscience.

 

just pure ego. do not ask for mercy for them.

 

they are not deserving.

 

 

no dialogue is possible

 

when either there to harm,

or for their pride.

 

no giving mercy.

 

evil begins there.

or always present.
