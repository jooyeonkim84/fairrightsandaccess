These people, my extended family.

 

They are creeping up on gaining power,

 

through leverage. Making me into a topic of discussion.

 

And antagonizing me by stating false pride,

 

false virtue signaling,

 

false moral superiority.

 

While they are in the wrong.

 

They were tribal, they alienated a person with their prejudice.

 

They deserve no reward, but punishment.

 

 

That I say in absolute fairness.

 

 

These are deceptive, insidious people.
