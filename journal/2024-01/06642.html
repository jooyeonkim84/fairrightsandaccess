As for these people, the few who are convicted.

Their crimes are relentless, repeating, in pursuit of harm.

Why whatever, whichever punishment, is justifiable.

Victim cannot be targeted throughout his life.

Because of their pursuit.

And without remorse.

 

Not saying so as personal preference,

but by the law,

the law itself would recognize,

that someone is being targeted and going through living hell

that is crime in progress.

 

 

That remorseless, relentless, suppression of conscience,

aiming for harm.

 

That character and what they have done based on this,

must be taken into account.
