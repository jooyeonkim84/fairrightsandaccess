And IF I am a prospectful King,

that does not give you the right to hold me in this way.

That is OUR affairs.

It is another territory you are INVADING.

 

End this.

 

In the eyes of the law. I am a civilian.

I have been attacked by your citizens.

They were exploiting the fact that I am in the blind,

and have been given many advantages,

possibly, if I am not wrong,

re-instating new judges,

and even deleting records.

I have been framed,

targeted,

and exploited business-wise,

as many abuse me for power.

And endlessly, it repeats.
