By the way,

 

when I joke around,

 

I am joking around not hurting anyone.

 

 

It is not a permission for anyone to

 

latch on to that humor

 

and start targeting me, with an harmful intent.

 

 

likewise jokes are fine,

 

but where you originate,

 

and where you take that to which realm.

 

 

 

that will count.

 

 

kindly keep an look out for such people.

 

 

they are just searching for such opportunity,

 

why i can't even joke around these days.
