You people have no idea

 

how time consuming and difficult

 

it is to be interpreting,

 

refining, metaphorical relays.

 

 

for.

 

 

5. consecutive. relentless.

 

 

YEARS.

 

 

i am not a functional human being.

 

 

i'm a cripple barely surviving.

 

 

 

that is not a joke.

 

 

why am i stuck in here like this?

 

 

what the hell is the point?

 

and why am i on public display still?

 

 

why is this the circus?
