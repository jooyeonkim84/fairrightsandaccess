Not a fair thing.

 

They get to see my work,

 

while I can't see theirs.

 

I have nothing to compare myself to,

 

and grow from that comparison.

 

 

So give me hints to what is going on

 

or let me in.

 

 

If i'm going to get chewed out eventually,

 

then at least let me have my sight.

 

 

 

If the "3" is my extended family.

 

And if they attacked my work,

 

following up on my ideas,

 

and for what they aimed,

 

they should be charged and yet.

 

They are rewarded?
