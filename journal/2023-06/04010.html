He may sound civil,

 

until you even request something reasonable,

and he must do it against the person's will.

 

he may sound level-headed,

but that completely changes depending on what he allows

in himself,

 

and that's not much.

 

doesn't take much to turn this guy

into a lunatic.

 

 

what is he getting charged for,

drug exchange? cocaine?

 

I don't know.

 

 

What I do know is that,

this guy, seemingly friendly one moment,

can suppress conscience in an instant the next,

 

without the fear of repercussions that follow.

 

 

Don't dismiss his behavior at the shelter as well.
