What do you do,

 

 

when EVERY person,

 

 

even your supposed supporters or allies.

 

 

 

They are in on this.

 

 

 

 

They strip you of your rights.

 

 

 

And they believe this is funny.

 

 

 

 

 

Do you believe that angering me,

 

 

do you think that scarring me.

 

 

 

 

This will just disappear?

 

 

 

Do you think that lightly of me?

 

 

 

Is your prejudice coming from misjudgment of my comic character?

 

 

 

 

 

Why do you laugh at this situation?
