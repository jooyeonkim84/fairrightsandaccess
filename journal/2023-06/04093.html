Please do not forget the persisting abuse

even after the authorities settled the matter between us,

and reconciliation between us,

when I was trying to help this guy.

 

He went on with these attacks, out of his own

psychological complex of some kind.

 

Of course I would be insanely frustrated.

 

He wouldn't let me sleep. Deliberately waking me up constantly.

I was becoming very ill with vertigo, inner ear, which expanded

down to my arm.

 

His anger was out of the bounds.

 

And he thinks he has the right to speak out constantly,

 

he aims for defamation.

 

He should be charged.
