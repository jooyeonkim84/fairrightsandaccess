The psychology of my sister is

 

something that wants to be domineering.

 

something that wants to believe someone (me)

is weaker, or becoming weak, or is broken.

 

or something like this.

 

and she thrives on the idea.

 

as you can see from her past deeds.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

a pitiful, shallow creature.

 

both of them. like-minded people live together always.

 

 

 

 

they cannot be excused for all their evil.
