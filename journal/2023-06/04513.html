I can't live a single day without feeling anxiety

and feeling fear.

 

When the Royal family had so much power,

yet they expect this, me protesting,

still allowing many harm.

 

 

Do I have any safety at all

friend or foe?

 

 

Should you not have been protecting me from harm?

 

 

Should you not have been more careful in how you would

treat my emotions, that is already vulnerable?

 

 

Sigh...

 

 

 

Where do I go, when everybody.

 

Everybody.

 

Is there to see me as a target of some kind.

 

 

Find it amusing.

 

 

When you are in fact, destroying me....

 

 

 

Now I don't know where to turn. My spirit is lost.

 

 

Why is everyone doing this to me?

 

 

Do you have mercy on everyone else BUT me?
