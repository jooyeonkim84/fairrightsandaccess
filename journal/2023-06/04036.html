So exactly why should I benefit everyone,

when I am relentlessly abused like this.

 

Mocked and laughed at?

 

 

 

Where is this all going?

 

 

 

Happy end?

 

 

Giving up to your manipulation, your control, your choices leading to effects

in my life of which I am fully aware?

 

 

 

 

If one single presence of authority, whether Mr Musk, or King Charles.

 

 

Would help.

 

 

 

In a way I can protect myself. I can live a life without worrying about

 

all this.

 

 

 

But you deliberately keep me like this don't you? For your ultimate benefit.

 

Whatever that goal is.

 

 

It's endless control.
