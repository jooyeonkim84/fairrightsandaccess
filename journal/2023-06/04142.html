One of the main issues is that people,

 

most seemingly everyone,

 

sees me as something to affect and control,

 

something to toy with, as a pushover or some kind.

 

 

 

 

Issue also is that, it is a great underestimation

 

and situationally, idiotic on their part.

 

 

Of all things that is going on.

 

You are at a disadvantage.

 

Yet you persist.

 

 

 

Whether short-term or long,

 

it will return to you.

 

 

 

I am someone who believes in answers.

 

And that answer is an reaction,

 

it always will be.

 

 

 

Be the cause, get the reaction.

 

 

 

No idea why Steve isn't incarcerated already.

 

 

Should losing control my only communication?

 

 

Why are people allowed to get a rise out of me?

 

 

For no particular reason other than catering to their own ego.

 

 

Why is that allowed?
