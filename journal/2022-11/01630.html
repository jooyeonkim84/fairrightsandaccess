As for helping Twitter....

 

 

 

Been walking back and forth to the mall thinking about this.

 

 

If to analyze what Twitter's strength is about,

and if there are any features that is lacking,

 

 

I believe Twitter is famous for its speedy delivery in connecting to the mass.

Why the text is limited, and that text is distributed.

 

 

But it may be lacking a component. Or missing its potential of connecting that

text into something more practical, functional, tangible.

 

Meaning, if to use the text in such a way that it becomes a commandline,

(much like coding, but refined for convenience),

 

across many fields, such as.... payment, IoT, upload/download archive, streaming services...

 

for instance,

 

#ipay + #tosomeone "do you want $340 at 11pm?"

 

and the message goes to this person, and they click accept within the twitter window.

And the transaction is instantly completed.

 

commandline #ipay + #tosomeone "do you want $340 at 11pm"

 

is actually only 4 variables required.

 

first #ipay + #tosomeone must come in,

then they can insert $amount + (number) am/pm, and that message will be sent to the person.

 

Such feature could be added for 1 dollar a month (micropayment)

and it would save quite a bit of time, logging in and out of certain places.....

 

 

or, appliances/computers could be commanded with such commandlines,

#securitycameraon at 4pm = turns on the security camera pre-installed at home showing up on twitter window.

#secuiritycameraoff at 6pm = following. etc. command sent through more popular devices. like alexa? or some BT device...

 

likewise....... content that is shared within Twitter is automatically synched to other places as organization.

and brought forth with the commandline.

 

hmm............

 

what Twitter could be lacking.... beyond that is........

 

people taking chances on the matching algorithm -

there isn't enough activity, the necessity people go through everyday.

 

like buying/selling. asking questions. blogs.

maybe a survey for these algorithms. interest coverage.

 

assigning meaning to data they upload.

so people try out their luck. those features could be added to the side panel....... (i'm generalizing but i'm sure there can be many ways)

 

so what i'm saying is.

 

centralization of everyday activity at the convenience and speed of a commandline text. (Action to effect) (right content panel)

and for more meaningful content, use matching algorithms in different areas in life that is needed. (Research to discovery) (left content panel)

 

so everyday activity usage through commandline = micropayments.

user created content = matching algorithms for discovery, suggestions. in various fields. (free)

 

then through these data, using Twitter as a centralized location for everyday activity.

 

eventually one encounters problems in their community that needs to be solved,

that should be collected in channel, and then my data segmentation website comes in. xD
