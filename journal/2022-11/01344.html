Whatever that they were doing,

it had stopped for a while I believe.

 

It was continued during the times I was rising again.

My success is what triggers them to destroy me personally.

 

 

 

How can I live, when I am a target?

Every movement I make, I am attacked behind my back.

When I am in the blind, when I cannot do anything to defend myself.

 

That in it itself is their crime,

but their actions, their aim, their goals.

 

It is far more destructive and evil than one can imagine.
