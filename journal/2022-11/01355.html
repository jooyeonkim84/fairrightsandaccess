the spherical 'destructo ball'

would have its advantages

if the physics of my imagination

is valid in reality.

 

1. no chemical usage.

can be re-used over and over

again. repair is also easy,

so as long as the inner components

aren't damaged. (casting metal

in the same mold)

 

2. size is only relative to the

strength of the inner mechanism.

that strength is relative to

its size and power of the motor.

it is not limited.

 

3. cutting the space escalator in half,

one can build a foundation for a

giant destructo ball. which can

destroy an entire city within days....
