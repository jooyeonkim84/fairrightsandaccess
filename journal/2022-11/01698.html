There is much I want to share and help,

to Prince William and Prince Harry.

 

I know that is what the Queen would have wanted.

 

I see glimpses of what tabloid says about

their distance.

 

And I hope that is not as bad as they say it is.

But it looks grim. It looks serious.

 

I feel heavy, concerned. I also understand

that it may not be in my place to intervene.

 

I do not wish to incur both your wrath.

With my ignorance.

 

But know that I mean well.

And I wish both you well.
