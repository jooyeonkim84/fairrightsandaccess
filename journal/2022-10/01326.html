Ultimately, I think 'intent' should be seen here

seeing the flow of everything.

 

And that 'intent' is....

very constant. Harm.

 

Intent to harm, from hatred and anger,

is very constant.

 

Through whichever ways.

 

Though he hides the intent and backstabs instead....

 

It was, self-indulgence, rebellion to satisfy himself.

In everything. If he had cared for the well-being

of another, none of this would have surfaced.

 

 

From my end, I feel like a victim to someone

who is aggressively selfish by nature.

 

Someone who is ruthless of his actions,

and finds every possible excuse.
