And I am a ghost to you.

As you are a ghost to me.

All I see are relays. Not actual faces, not actual words.

 

But the main difference between yourselves and I:

 

You have the power of tangibility; tangible effect.

 

I am stuck in this virtual realm, completely reliant on your confirmation.

 

 

And all this.

 

ALL that antagonization.

 

 

It all reflects on YOU.
