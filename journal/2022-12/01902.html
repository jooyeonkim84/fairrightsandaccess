And people, don't get the wrong idea

that i'm this uptight, angry, person.

 

i'm usually not. i'm rather friendly -

 

it's just that when i see these attacks,

and injustice that follow neglecting my

well-being, despite the on-going harm,

 

 

i have no choice but to make a statement.

 

 

and in this case,

 

 

i have to write over and over again,

 

because people exploit the fact that i am in the blind,

 

and they, instead helping to refine the wrong,

 

allow it to happen, hardly respecting me as a person.

 

 

 

i hope you can understand.

 

 

 

but in reality, i'm actually not this anger-driven person.

 

i am friendly - and i tend to be a 'nice' person,

 

generally speaking.
