If my sister is talking about food again,

 

note that I eat meat, my cousins know this.

 

My sister, as far as I know, is a picky eater

and do not eat meat or certain types of meat.

 

She keeps saying she didn't have enough to

eat because I ate it all.

 

We weren't rich but we had plenty to eat

one way or another.

 

In fact, there was a time in high school,

where I deliberately tried to lose weight

by starving. To look better.

 

I think I relied on few slices of watermelon

a day for nearly several weeks, and my mom

was terrified of that incident. She was always

afraid I might go into starvation mode again.

 

 

The only times I took food from her

was snacks she hid in her room (like chocolates)

which I believe I ate maybe 6 times in total

(in Canada).

 

So either that has happened few times

or she's greatly exaggerating.

said on the channel. I believe she said I 'slept'

with my mother? You insane cunt. Sigh.....

when it comes to anger.

in this situation.
