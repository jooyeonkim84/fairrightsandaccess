To be fighting, fending off attacks

while being blind.

 

Only glimpses of relays,

and by intuition,

prediction...

 

This in it itself is very wrong,

and you will know their intent based on this.

 

If people seek fairness,

they would not attack someone who is in the blind.

 

It is an attempt for something else,

and their actions on the channel would prove their

character.
