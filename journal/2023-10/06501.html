And by the way, because I fight against so many one may think I am petty.

 

I am not.

 

 

First once or twice, I am on the forgiving side. I try to be.

 

I try to reason.

 

 

 

But what I do not understand is,

 

where that relentless pursuit is coming from.

 

 

That impossible antagonization and disrespect.

 

Intent to harm.
