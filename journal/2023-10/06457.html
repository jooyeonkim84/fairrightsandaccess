you can yap all you want by words,

 

as if you are all supporting.

 

 

but the reality is, you plainly see me suffer.

 

you keep me isolated.

 

and you raise the bar trying to get more out of me,

 

 

while i am given nothing.

 

while i am sacrificed.

 

 

what protection i see?

 

infinite humiliation.

 

 

at your fucked up excuses.

 

 

you raise up my anger. and if i were to express,

 

you'll use that against me to keep me here longer,

 

 

and you create that cycle.

 

 

 

get me out. that's enough.

 

what are you people doing?
