I shiver at the capacity of malice, control, and hostility, and callousness that is present everywhere.

The world and its people was far different from I was led to believe.

I never knew how dark people were.

And how easily they can jump into destruction without a second thought as their initiation.

It is as if the law doesn't exist.

Nobody cares about it.

As long as you can fool it.

Persuade it.

 

Then control is justified.

No matter what.

 

 

This is pursuit of harm.

And they must be charged for it.

They must be stopped from it.
