Trap or not.

Expressive or not.

 

It all leads to one path.

 

Every word written,

every thought expressed.

 

All leads to the path of logic, reason, truth.

In the majority.

 

And you can't deny that as much as you can't deny flow of time.

Whatever permanence nature holds,

so is what is engraved in our minds as the truth.

 

And yeah, your ego shields it.

But you reveal yourselves simultaneously, ever been, as well.

It comes with hostility.

Who are you really fighting but yourselves?

 

Listen to your conscience, drop your ego.

And speak things as they are.

Stop trying to destroy me.

 

I have never been your enemy.
