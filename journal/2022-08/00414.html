air conditioned well in the library.

incredibly sleepy but hanging on.

getting off the meds as much as possible.

 

overall, becoming healthy.

no particular issues but.......

trouble is bound to arrive at some point.

 

why this morning, some guy cuts in front of the line

at breakfast. of all the people. why me?

do i look like a pushover?

 

I question, he moves out of the line.

a game of dominance this is.

 

the goal here is to collect reference.

and branch out.
