For those who are still talking about my mom.

There is a difference between statement and subjugation.

You are repeating yourself in order to affect,

in the worst of ways. When there is no need to repeat.

Authorities must carefully observe the intent of these people.

Most of their reactions were negative when I requested help

at the hospital. Pessimism, superiority over intellect,

win or lose in this sense, a game of ego may be at play.

Consider the flow of all things said, how it repeats,

and if it is coming from a place to repeat harassment.
