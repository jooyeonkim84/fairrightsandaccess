Do people understand what it's like to

be monitored by the public 24/7?

 

And you have no way of defending your privacy

because you don't have any money?

 

You become what you imagine,

and if the public decides this for you....

That if one must live up to the expectations of the mass.

Their destiny follows the crowd.

Not the other way around.

You forfeit control over your life.

 

If there be no ways to find your soverignity

even within your own mind....

 

Where is this person?
