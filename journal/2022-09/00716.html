Mr Gates. Mr Bezos.

 

Kindly say a few words to him in private.

 

Both of you know, he stepped over the line

one too many times and enjoyed the process of it.

 

While I know it is my loss financially.

When and how that money would have arrived,

through what abuse and harassment is another question.

 

I have some respect for myself and I cannot work

with someone like this.
