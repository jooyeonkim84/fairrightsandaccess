In all fairness,

 

just ask him to stop.

 

 

 

Stop insinuating messages like this.

 

 

And if he repeats despite the authorities having asked,

 

 

then you would clearly know that he is being abusive.

 

 

 

If not now.

 

 

 

 

But when you don't get involved,

 

 

does my requests have any meaning?

 

 

Will he not deliberately ignore this?

 

 

And would that be an offense in your eyes?

 

 

No.

 

 

 

Evil is in when you see something happening in repeat,

 

in the open,

 

and when the person is suffering because of it,

 

you choose to do nothing.
