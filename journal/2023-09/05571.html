My spending little money to make my ends meet,

does not reflect on my financial education,

or how I would handle my money.

 

Do not correlate this as you are forcing it.

First, I am spending to make my basic needs met.

My even the most basic needs are not met.

 

Things people take for granted, I do not have.

 

I've been an reader of Robert Kiyosaki

since my teenage years, alongside marketing,

some level of product development.

 

So you can bet that even with 'little' amounts

of money, I would spend it better than yourselves

in terms of investment, should it be a lump sum.

 

If enough. So please do not judge based on that.

 

It's like you see me as a child,

as you see me in the weakest of state.

 

But why do you all see me in here and judge me based

on my reaction to this hardship?
