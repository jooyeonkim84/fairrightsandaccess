There is the criminal,

Here is the victim,

and you are the witnesses

 

Mr Musk.

 

Law is the law,

and it doesn't matter what I want,

I am only reciting the law.

 

Do not blame me for this.

 

For all that pursuit of harm,

to someone unsuspecting, unexpecting such hostilities,

he deserves this.

 

There is a certain give and take,

even between enemies.

 

This relentless pursuit,

This hatred and prejudice,

This was uncalled for.

 

 

What if someone was on pursuit of destroying your life,

and showed predatory behavior based on who they

think you are?

 

From your end, it's merely entertainment.

 

From the receiving end. This is horror.
