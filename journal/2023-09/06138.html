King Charles.

 

 

I understand where you are coming from.

 

That you don't want me to indulge into

anger, and be a 'better' person.

 

But King Charles. I know these people.

 

 

They are something relentless and

dark by nature. They operate from

place of ego. These are people

who may show cowardice in the moment

of need, but will go back to their

harmful form one way or another.

 

 

They are not something to be taken

lightly.
