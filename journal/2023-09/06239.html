In high school. When I was to study for a test,

during important times.

 

I remember my father dealt with a friend

and had him bring in this giant router machine,

to the backyard.

 

It made impossible noise. And I couldn't think

at all.

 

 

It's all about yourselves. Nevermind the person.

 

Your opinions matter more. Your say is bigger than mine.

 

Despite the fact that I am the victim.
