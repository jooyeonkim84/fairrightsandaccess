Sigh... Seeing the relays,

 

My sister is lying if she's saying that my mother committed suicide.

 

Only hours before her death, we were chatting by text, my mother and I,

 

and I sent her a comic book she's read as a child, somehow I found this

 

over the Korean search engine, and she was extremely excited about it.

 

Not to mention she sighed in relief as I was talking to her as she was on the hospital bed,

 

waiting. That she'll be okay because we caught it early and everyone survives even

 

after aneurysm burst if within few hours. I was trying to comfort her...

 

 

 

My sister is an evil person. I mean evil to the core.

 

She will say this to affect me, to blame me, to curse me.

 

As she has attempted before in slander.

 

Such as saying I have slept with my mother.

 

She is a lowlife without conscience.

 

Please understand this.

 

 

 

 

She has also contradicted trying to shift the blame to father.

 

Some kind of argument has happened between them,

 

and her blood pressure has risen.

 

It is likely she was the initiator, and is hiding this.

 

And is sending blame elsewhere.
