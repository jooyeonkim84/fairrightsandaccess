In every business or academia,

 

when you do well enough,

 

you are supported in some form.

 

One way or another.

 

Instead, it just gets worse and worse,

 

people just there to attack me constantly.

 

 

 

Do you really think these are the ways to have someone

 

reaching their potential?

 

 

 

Give me something. Anything.

 

I can't live like this.
