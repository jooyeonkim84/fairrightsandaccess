Retractable pillars were a result of my playing with Sketchup.

Making of cubes and protruding or retracting them as a feature.

It follows the direct principles of the Space Escalator and in the same program.

And the isometric game, "Tactics Ogre", of which I was thinking of. (They are all in cubes)

 

It was first presented as a way to create artificial filming sets.

Just like in the video game.  

Of which i've drawn in sketch up.

However, unlike the video game, curvatures would need to be placed on top of those cubes.

Whether be it ground, or as roofs. Which would need adaptations and machining with wood/metal, and even dirt/grass, etc.

But most of the volume that is required would be covered, and ever-morphing into the shapes and height people need as ground work.

Cameras are are usually placed on top above a ceiling isolated from the pillars, as the pillars are enwrapped inside an outside frame. (For the cameras) For the pillars, where there are no roofs, that can work as and advantage as most filming sets do not have roofs.

 

So that was the first suggestion.

Then I changed the pillars into greenscreen pillars, maybe 6 to 1 year later.

So to cover the volume of the set people walking around in certain dimensional axis and height, but rest everything can be CGI.
