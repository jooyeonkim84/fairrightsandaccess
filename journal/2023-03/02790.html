I can choose to go anywhere I please, had I the money.

I can choose to sever my ties with the internet itself, close the laptop.

 

But if I do so,

 

Will the monitoring stop?

Will the public displays stop?

Will I know what is going on behind my back?

Will I know what type of disinformation, propaganda to began in my absence?

Will I know if there will be threats? Harm?

Will there be restrictions to the monitoring? Hacking the bathroom camera,

had it the chance, and release it on public display? Audio?

 

 

If that is not violation of human rights, and captivity by cyber-bullying, what is?

 

 

Besides the above, what of the harm attempted offline?

 

When they had no jurisdiction.

 

When they already knew how it would affect me.

 

What of the nature of all those crimes? Can it be justified with some excuses

when it began from a place of harm? When their citizens attacked me?

And the same things have been used, but only enhanced?

 

Those were in their right?

 

 

I don't think so.

 

 

These are all personalized attacks of which they had no excuse over.

 

 

None of this is right.
