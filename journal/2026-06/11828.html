I believe,

and I am not trying insult you.

But I think top authority is naive.

When you judge based on projected character,

instead their actions.

Which explains what they are.

They have a way with words, deception and justification.

But under no circumstance, this is acceptable to already a vulnerable person.

Who was lured by their promised prospects, and killed for the contribution.

And in their self-reasoning.

And Zuckerberg, Elon Musk, or whomever individuals, aren't getting away with they've done.

 

 

If this is to be a political game,

where you do not care for any criminality,

but you want to assert power.

I will learn from that.

Kindly, let there be fairness, as I believed in the law to be.

Instead status and power ruling over morality.
