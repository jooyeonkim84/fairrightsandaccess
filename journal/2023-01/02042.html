Further she tried raising mom's BP going attempting to go inside

her room after the first brain surgery.

 

Over money.

 

 

But in reality, it was the factor long past.

 

That mother favored me over her.

 

That she held an impossible grudge.

 

 

 

 

Another instance.

 

I had a Mac Pro, somewhat expensive computer.

 

When my sister came over,

 

the desktop accidentally fell on the floor and was damaged in its handle.

 

 

 

Suddenly, my sister is singing in Korean, in repeat. Not from a song,

but one that is made up by herself.

 

She kept repeating with a insane gloat on her face,

 

"I am a happy person" ("나는 행복 합니다~ 나는 행복 합니다~)

 

5 or 6 times, she seemed to be in this 'trance' type of state of mind.

 

Before, the moment she saw the desktop her face just distorted momentarily

in agony.

 

The subtle nuance of her insanity, the jealousy, the violence, the hostility,

along with her husband, was out of the bounds, and it became stronger

and stronger as mom got ill and we were at their mercy.
