It has always been in the realm of civility and fair reasoning by open dialogue.

However,

starting with subjugation of my rights by sight and full access to the court,

 

they have exploited and framed me in unimaginable ways.

 

Knowing that I am blind.

 

 

When that very situation is figured out.

 

That they have exploited my blindness.

 

 

Should you the court, continue to allow this to happen?

 

No. You do not hold that right. You never have.

 

 

Please let me in.

 

 

Save me from the troubles of trying to get in there.

Of which, I am not entirely certain what the outcome will be.

 

You must give me my rights.
