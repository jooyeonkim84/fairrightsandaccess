As part of my chicken/farming/self-sustenance series,

 

I thought about a quick and painless,

if not somewhat automated way to kill the chicken

individually.

 

Not in an assembly line.

 

Apparently they use a metallic 'rectangle cone' that

they stick the neck out, and... kill the chicken with a

knife.

 

But the blood gets everywhere,

the chicken often gets out of the cone,

 

so I thought,

 

1) make it a circular cone.

 

2) when you see a camera, when it adjusts

its 'aperture'(?) there is a circular mechanism

that is divided in many slides, closes in to

form a smaller or bigger circle. Like the iris

in a person's eye. It changes itself to allow

the amount of light coming in and out of the

lens.

 

3.) If this mechanism was thicker,

it can hold the chicken inside the cone,

adjusting to the shape and size of the chicken.

 

which also would hold the neck portion as well.

 

4.) and the last portion would be like a

sideway guillotine. which can be triggered

when the user is in distance, so not to get

blood over them......

 

 

it's a gruesome thing. but this is also

painless for the chicken instead people using

knives.
