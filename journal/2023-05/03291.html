note mr musk,

any of my past acquaintances,

making use of the space escalator,

in order to suggest something big.

all that volume, whatever it is,

comes after my work and following through it.

either my permission or no go.

if ill-acquaintance, no way.

and kindly wait until my ideas.

i've yet to branch out.

 

generally, kindly don't deal with my enemies.

when you see what they've done to leverage into the situation.

i become a stepping stone, and their crimes are not yet punished.

perhaps this is because you see them as one of the competitors and showing interest.

don't make my life a hell. thanks.
