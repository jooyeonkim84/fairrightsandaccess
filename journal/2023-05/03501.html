And King Charles,

 

recently I am seeing no adoption relays again.

But please note that depending on what you say,

 

as my status or power may decrease,

 

 

so can the abuse increase.

So whether you mean it or not, it is irrelevant.

stop my suffering, and yet you insist on this

 

pushing someone to the edge.
