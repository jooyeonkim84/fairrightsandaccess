I know how easily, easily, people can lose themselves

 

in the heat of anger and lose everything they built up.

 

 

And what i'm trying to do, is to prevent something like

 

this from escalating, perhaps due to misunderstanding,

 

or something that needs to be corrected by civilized

 

dialogue and understanding.

 

 

 

But what I do know is that, any sort of passive aggression,

 

is not the direction we should be heading towards.

 

 

 

There are far better ways, and this certainly,

 

whatever it is, should not be about that.

 

 

 

I think there are people in our lives that correlates to

 

such hostility back and forth, and justifiably so,

 

but this, in this instance, it's just indulgence to

 

vanity. Giving up to self-conjured negativity and emotions.

 

 

I cannot be part of this. Please understand.
