One more thing.

 

 

If I do get prepared and deliver.

 

 

 

Do you think with all the creativity permitted in the world,

 

 

and I, being in recognition of this.

 

 

 

Do you think there'd be evidence?

 

 

 

 

 

No. There wouldn't be.

 

 

 

And the person who is capable of doing that,

 

is tempted by that idea.

 

 

 

Because it is in their power to do so.
