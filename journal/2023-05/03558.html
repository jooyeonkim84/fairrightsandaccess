I don't know if the TNG cast, along with their

script writers know that they really are the --- heroes of our time.

 

I don't know what it was like in the States during the 80's and 90's.

 

But in Korea, that kind of rationality, logic, better

example of human behavior - it was rare during those times.

 

At least in my world anyway. Against impossible

domestic violence and something opposite to rationality.

Ignorance, tyranny and force. All about the ego,

and evil that followed.

 

And growing up, I turned to TNG quite a bit.

The show set my principles and raised me in a way.

 

Not that I turned out so exemplary, but it gave me

a chance to believe in the future.

 

So my hats off to the TNG cast and the writers.

 

and it really was an honor to have been part of that world.

 

Be it inside a television screen. It was real to me. :)

 

 

 

Wish I was a better writer and in better state of mind

to express my gratitude. The show was hope. It was

about my faith in better people and better life.

I didn't know until I got older. :)
