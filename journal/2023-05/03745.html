People buying into that they are good people.

 

that they are sheeps.

 

 

 

they were evil to ME.

 

 

not others.

 

 

but to ME.

 

 

 

 

that repeat and that variation.

 

 

 

 

as I stood doing nothing.

 

 

 

 

 

But to be facing harshest moments of life.

 

 

 

 

 

Understand this.

 

 

 

Don't buy into what they say.

 

 

The evil is in their own self-reasoning.

 

 

That point in ambiguity of right and wrong they choose to stay,

 

 

 

until they act on it.

 

 

 

 

And repeats, to their self-reasoning, infinite number of times.

 

 

 

Focus on what was done.

 

 

Blood donation? Impersonal.

 

 

They do good to others,

 

 

but when it is a person they know.

 

 

 

What do you see?

 

 

 

List the facts.

 

 

 

 

That is where the danger is.

 

 

 

That self-reasoning evil, that ambiguity they deliberately choose to

stay.

 

 

A child getting beat up by his father,

 

they stand and watch.

 

 

 

 

As a group, it's always easy to suppress conscience.

 

 

 

That is what they are.
