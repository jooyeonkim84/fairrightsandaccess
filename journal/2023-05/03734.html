If David is moving out,

 

please pay the landlord rent for a year,

 

and don't have upstairs occupied with a tenant

other than myself.

 

 

The issue is that someone out there keeps referring everyone

I ever meet, or going to meet into that channel.

 

 

And everyone invests into it emotionally.

 

 

And in turn, it becomes expressive towards me,

and I become a target whether I am trying to be nice or not.

 

 

This has been my experience everywhere I go.

 

 

Just kindly, leave me be.
