When these people, when they're not refined.

 

As I fight back,

 

The people passing by everyday,

 

they have their opinions about it as well.

 

 

They don't say a word, but it is expressed in series of gestures.

 

Sometimes angry, sometimes kind (perhaps that is default). Whichever side they're on.

 

 

But you can certainly 'feel' the message they're trying to get across when they're mad.

 

 

Though I don't even know them, I have nothing against them personally.

 

 

Watch out. You don't know the details. You can only be assuming.

 

 

Stay neutral. Please don't make my day more difficult than it already is.

 

 

I am but a stranger. Please understand this.

 

 

If anyone is doing this.

 

And they are.
