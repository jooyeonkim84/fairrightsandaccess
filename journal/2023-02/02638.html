One thing that should not be tolerated.

 

Is trying to instill fear into a person by force.

 

Physical force.

 

If it does.

 

 

There is a problem.

 

 

That fear remains.

 

 

And that person will be haunted by it.

 

 

Until he decides to overcome that fear.

 

 

With whatever force necessary.

 

 

What do you think is stronger?

 

 

That momentary anger, or inevitable intent?

 

 

If you aren't going to end the person,

 

don't even start it.

 

 

It repeats in a cycle.

 

 

If one is going to fight, be respectful about it.

 

 

Be fair about it.

 

 

No one is invincible.

 

 

 

 

Understand in the end, it's motivation that wins. The intent that wins.

 

Not anyone's physical prowess.

 

 

 

 

IF anyone there is making threats right now, not as self-defense,

or gloating, without proper give and take.

 

That should be taken into account.
