You may also be thinking,

 

any publicity is better than no publicity.

 

 

What does this kid, coming up from bottom rung of society

got to lose?

 

 

You make this choice at my expense,

 

then you are not treating me as a person.

 

 

This would be a type of discrimination,

derogation at your own reasoning.

 

What is offense and abuse,

distortion of my image,

 

and I, having to fight for this.

 

You allow that conflict.

 

 

I cannot forgive this.

 

Please understand.
