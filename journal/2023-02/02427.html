What of my sister and her husband?

 

If they were let go, and you still chose to work with me,

 

despite my statement.

 

 

If you believe you have the rights to use my plans.

 

 

What is this?

 

 

 

 

Is the King, in his religious view,

 

that compassion is the only answer.

 

 

So he intervenes the law to free my attackers,

 

while using me for his gain?

 

 

I am sacrificed because of his beliefs,

 

I am sacrificed for himself?

 

 

Is this what is happening?
