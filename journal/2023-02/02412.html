For me, I tried blocking it with tapes, as I did not know

if I am allowed to use a screwdriver on the walls, or nail it.

 

It held for a while and then fell off.

 

Because my bed is in the corner side of the room,

I did not expect myself to be seen from the window.

 

But apparently people can see.

 

Whether there is a camera on the streets

or if it's coming from opposite side of the road,

from one of the buildings I do not know.

 

I can't detect the cameras.

 

But I would like to know why it took so long for the person

to install the blinds. Not the person who came here today

to install it, but the original person who promised me

the blinds and disappeared to all this duration.
