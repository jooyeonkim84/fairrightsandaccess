Know this.

 

I am only human.

 

 

I only react to what I believe in is the right thing.

 

 

 

What I believe is part of the reason for existence.

 

 

 

And I have always been exposed of this.

 

 

 

 

There are people exploit this fact.

 

 

 

 

That is evil.

 

 

 

Don't be evil.

 

 

 

You don't do this to your worst enemy.

 

 

 

I am at your worst, just a competitor.

At your best, an ally. A potential benefactor.
