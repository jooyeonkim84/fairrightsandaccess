My opinion on the capital punishment is this.

I think it depends on whether they are capable of repent, remorse, regret.

For what they've done to cause suffering.

Because otherwise, they mean to repeat.

They mean to create cycles, and escalate.

Why support to see that happening?

 

It is not as callous as it sounds.

Rather from the victim's end,

they *must be callous,

if they want no more wars in their lives.

 

But when they go on.

And it is determined that they can only feign themselves

so to mislead, for their advantages in that moment.

Only to be on pursuit of harm following.

They don't deserve it.

They don't deserve to exist.
