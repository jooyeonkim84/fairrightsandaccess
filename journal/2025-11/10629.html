sheldon has to choose mit or caltech.......

 

authorities.....

if you had the power to rise answers to issues,

from the littlest hints of solutions....

in anything you choose what would that be?

(not that i'm completely this)

 

i think the logical choice in an very finite lifespan,

and even as one may can bring about solutions,

knowing that it would take time,

i'd focus on life extension.

 

wouldn't you?

sure one can do other things as well.

but to confine someone to duties that requires devotion.

that requires constant attention and solutions to,

sadly, from evil or ego in people,

and have me endure this,

with a fake smile,

a courteous handshake,

hidden intent and planning against adversaries,

 

 whereas another life is one that of purpose and meaning.

not bound by status quo.

nor network that follows.

 

I can help the world far better without assigned roles.

So long as you decide to hear me out and announce me correctly.

And address my attackers and stop them from repeating harm.

 

So no. I don't think I need to choose anything.

And after all this,

I need therapy.
