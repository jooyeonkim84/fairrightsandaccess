What I don't get about some people in the

bottom rung of society.

 

There is a time and place and worthy cause

to risk yourself in throwing your life away.

 

But for many of them, every cause is worth the risk.

Every conflict, big or small, is worthy enough

to turn it into a fight.

 

Most people, value themselves enough NOT to do

something like that. Or at least have a limiter.

 

But some idiots do not.

 

Every topic is a chance to throw their lives

in a whim. Is that bravery? Or completely

misguided idiocy?

 

 

  Avoid people like this.

 

But too frequent in bottom rung of society.

 

 

It's because. They don't have much to lose.

 

So they can act with abandon, vanity.
