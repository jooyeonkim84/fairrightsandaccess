Mr Musk.

 

Probably, though there is no guarantee in innovation,

if I didn't have such a headache with people and

if without worries of sustenance.

If I could fully focus on work and work only.

 

In this duration,

 

I probably ended up with a hundred ideas

and three hundred branching.

 

But is that necessarily a good thing?

 

As my value goes up,

 

the more attempts at control.

 

the more attempts at trying to affect my life for the worse.

 

subconsciously or consciously, it's about

wanting to affect me from above, where I cannot reach,

where, I am kept in blind, and that is leverage.

 

Friend, foe, doesn't matter. XD

 

Way human beings are wired it seems.

 

But some go as far as attempting to destroy the person

in different ways.

 

 

So what exactly should I be doing now?

 

Come up with more ideas only to see people try and destroy me?

 

Just focus solely on my sustenance and let this opportunity

slip by?

 

Do I even have a choice on the matter?

 

 

The logical step I think,

 

is either I am given the compensation for the damages

from my attackers, or Mr Musk, you send me support.

 

Then I can go about sustaining myself, inspite of these

adversaries. Because I have means to focus.

 

But it also sounds like, someone out there is

restricting contact with me, restricting you from

sending support as well.

 

Everyone is trying to keep me captive and monitored.

 

Why?

 

What is the point of this?

 

 

I need to be respected of my basic rights.

This needs to be spoken of. My captive situation.

 

The fact that I am deliberately being treated as a ghost.

 

No contact.

 

No support.

 

No compensations to damages.

 

Nothing.

 

 

It is manipulation and I will not be part of it.

 

 

So something has to happen now.

 

 

 

Because, as I come up with more,

 

everyone is made of greed.

 

 

They will try whatever is possible to either deter or

take from it.

 

And we've unfortunately put ourselves in this loophole.

 

This, infinite cycle of torture.

 

 

 

 

For me. And think about this statement carefully.

 

FOR ME. It only goes down ONE WAY.

 

It will become increasingly more difficult for me.

 

 

 

Rewards? A better life?

 

I will be permanently messed up before reaching that.

 

 

 

So what is the solution Mr Musk?

 

 

 

 

Don't you think you should brave up and send me funds,

 

so I can get out of here, as would any self-respecting person

would do?

 

At least have some amount of privacy?

 

And not worry about sustenance ending month after month?

 

 

 

 

What is the solution if you cannot help?
